Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging. Contact us to initiate a return.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days within Malaysia. Free shipping on orders over $80, otherwise $8.99 flat rate.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within Malaysia only. International shipping is coming soon — sign up to our newsletter to be notified first.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, they enter processing and changes may not be possible.

How do I track my order?

After placing your order, visit the Order page on our site to see your order history and status. You'll also get a confirmation email.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, FPX online banking, and Cash on Delivery. All card payments are securely processed.
